About The Traveling Tavern

The Traveling Tavern Experience

Beverages should be more than something served in a glass. They should tell a story.

At The Traveling Tavern, we believe a thoughtfully crafted cocktail can become part of a celebration, a signature mocktail can create a memorable experience for guests of all ages, and the smallest details can transform a simple drink into something unforgettable.

Our approach is rooted in hospitality, creativity, and intention. Every menu is thoughtfully designed around the event, the season, and the people being celebrated.

The Founder's Perspective

Crafted with Intention

Every memorable gathering has a moment.

A toast between newlyweds. Friends gathered around a beautifully crafted cocktail. A guest discovering a signature mocktail created specifically for the occasion.

The Traveling Tavern was founded on the belief that beverages should be more than refreshments—they should become part of the experience.

Drawing from years of hospitality, bartending, event service, and culinary preparation, I approach every menu with creativity, attention to detail, and a passion for thoughtful presentation.

From house-made syrups and fresh herbs to seasonal ingredients and custom drink design, every beverage is created with purpose.

Whether serving an intimate celebration, a wedding reception, or a corporate gathering, my goal remains the same: to create memorable beverage experiences that feel welcoming, elevated, and uniquely yours.

Chloe Dalrymple Founder, The Traveling Tavern

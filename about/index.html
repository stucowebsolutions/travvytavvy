Planning & Service

A beautiful experience begins with a well-planned one.

We work closely with each client to ensure the practical details are addressed well in advance of the event. Your vision guides our service.

01

Dry-Hire Bartending

Clients are responsible for purchasing and providing alcoholic beverages. We provide professional bartending, custom menu design, shopping guidance, and complete bar service. Non-alcoholic and elevated mocktail services are also available.

02

Travel

The Traveling Tavern proudly serves Central Georgia and the metropolitan Atlanta area. Events within 30 miles of Macon, Georgia are included in standard service pricing. Events beyond that range may be subject to a travel fee based on distance, travel time, lodging requirements, and logistics.

03

Responsible Service

Guest safety is always a priority. Service may be refused to any guest who appears visibly intoxicated, underage, or otherwise unable to consume alcohol responsibly.

04

Venue Requirements

Clients are responsible for ensuring venue requirements, permits, permissions, and approvals related to alcohol service have been obtained prior to the event.

05

Outdoor Events

Outdoor events require adequate shelter and a safe, level service area with access to electricity. A contingency plan is recommended for severe weather conditions.

06

Planning & Communication

Setup access, guest counts, menu selections, service requirements, and event logistics are confirmed during the planning process so every detail can be addressed well in advance.

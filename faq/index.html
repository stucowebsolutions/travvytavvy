What does dry-hire mean?
Dry-hire means The Travvy Tavvy provides the bartending service, beverage expertise, and event execution, while the client provides the alcohol.
Do you provide alcohol?
No. The Travvy Tavvy does not provide alcohol for any event.
What do you need for a quote?
Venue location, contact information, event date, guest count, event type, and a general idea of the beverage service needed.
Can I request a consultation through the website?
Yes. The site will include a consultation request and inquiry form so clients can begin the booking process online.
Do you have starting prices?
Yes, starting prices can be shown on the packages page, with final pricing customized based on the event details.

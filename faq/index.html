Service Basics

Understanding the experience.

A few of the most common questions about what The Traveling Tavern provides and what clients can expect from our service.

What is dry-hire bartending?

Dry-hire means The Traveling Tavern provides professional bartending services, custom menu design, bar setup, and service essentials while the client purchases and provides the alcohol. We handle everything else behind the bar.

Do you provide alcohol?

No. The Traveling Tavern operates as a dry-hire bartending service, so clients are responsible for purchasing and providing the alcoholic beverages served at their event.

We can provide shopping guidance to help you plan the beverage selection for your event.

Do you offer non-alcoholic options?

Absolutely. We specialize in elevated mocktails crafted with the same care and creativity as our cocktail menus. Full non-alcoholic packages are available for any event.

Do you provide the bar setup?

Yes. Our service includes a professionally styled bar setup tailored to your event aesthetic. We bring the barware, tools, garnishes, mixers, and non-alcoholic ingredients needed for service.

Can I customize my beverage menu?

Yes. Every menu is custom-designed around your event, taste, theme, season, and vision. Custom cocktails, mocktails, drink names, seasonal ingredients, and personalized menu design can all be incorporated into the experience.

Booking & Planning

Getting from inquiry to event day.

A little preparation goes a long way. These questions cover booking, guest counts, menus, and the information needed to start planning.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking as early as possible, particularly during peak wedding season from April through October.

Your date is not considered reserved until a signed agreement and retainer have been received.

What information do you need to provide a quote?

We typically need your venue location, contact information, event date, estimated guest count, event type, and a general idea of the beverage service you're looking for.

Additional details can be worked through during the planning process.

How many guests can you serve?

We serve events ranging from intimate gatherings of around 20 guests to large celebrations of 300 or more. Staffing can be scaled according to guest count to support seamless service throughout the event.

What happens after I submit an inquiry?

We'll use the information you provide to understand your event, beverage needs, and logistics. Questions and specific event details can be addressed during the planning process before finalizing service.

Your event date is reserved once the required agreement and retainer have been received.

Can I request a consultation through the website?

Yes. You can use the contact page to submit an inquiry and begin discussing your event, beverage service, and planning needs.

Travel & Logistics

The details behind the scenes.

Knowing the practical requirements ahead of time helps keep event day running smoothly.

What is your service area?

The Traveling Tavern proudly serves Central Georgia and the metropolitan Atlanta area.

Events within 30 miles of Macon, Georgia are included in standard service pricing. Events beyond that area may be subject to a travel fee based on distance, travel time, lodging requirements, and event logistics.

Destination events may be considered on a case-by-case basis.

What does an outdoor event need for the bar setup?

Outdoor events require adequate shelter and a safe, level service area. Access to electricity is also required for outdoor bar setups.

We recommend having a weather contingency plan in place for severe conditions and will work with you on event logistics during the planning process.

What are the client's responsibilities for the venue?

Clients are responsible for ensuring that the venue permits the planned beverage service and for obtaining any permits, permissions, approvals, insurance requirements, or other documentation required by the venue or applicable jurisdiction.

Do you need access to the venue before the event?

Reasonable access for setup, service, and breakdown is required. Setup times and specific access requirements will be confirmed during the planning process.

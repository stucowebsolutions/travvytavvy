01
Before You Book

Booking & Planning

How far in advance should I book? +

We recommend booking as early as possible, especially during peak wedding season from April through October. Your date is not reserved until a signed agreement and required retainer are received.

What information do you need to provide a quote? +

To build an accurate quote, we'll typically need your event date, venue location, approximate guest count, event type, contact information, and a general idea of the beverage experience you're looking for.

How do I reserve my event date? +

Your date becomes reserved once the required agreement has been signed and the retainer has been received. We'll walk you through the booking process after your event details and service needs have been discussed.

Can I customize my beverage menu? +

Absolutely. Every menu is custom-designed around your event. We can incorporate your preferences, theme, season, signature drinks, custom drink names, and personalized menu selections. Nothing is treated as an off-the-shelf experience.

02
Behind the Bar

Services & Experience

What is dry-hire bartending? +

Dry-hire means The Traveling Tavern provides the professional bartending service, custom menu design, bar setup, barware, tools, garnishes, mixers, and non-alcoholic ingredients while the client purchases and provides the alcohol.

Do you provide the alcohol? +

No. The Traveling Tavern operates as a dry-hire bartending service, so clients are responsible for purchasing and providing all alcoholic beverages served at their event.

We can provide shopping guidance and help you plan the quantities and selections needed for your menu.

Do you offer non-alcoholic options? +

Absolutely. We specialize in elevated mocktails crafted with the same care and creativity as our cocktail menus. Full non-alcoholic packages are available for events of all types.

Do you provide the bar setup? +

Yes. Our service includes a professionally styled bar setup tailored to the aesthetic of your event, along with the barware, tools, garnishes, mixers, and non-alcoholic ingredients needed for service.

How many guests can you serve? +

We serve events ranging from intimate gatherings of around 20 guests to large celebrations of 300 or more. Staffing and service requirements are scaled according to guest count and event logistics to support seamless service.

03
Event Logistics

Travel & Venue Requirements

What is your service area? +

The Traveling Tavern proudly serves Central Georgia and the Metropolitan Atlanta area. Events within 30 miles of Macon, Georgia are included in standard service pricing.

Events beyond the standard service area may be subject to a travel fee based on distance, travel time, lodging requirements, and event logistics. Destination events are considered on a case-by-case basis.

Are there additional travel fees? +

Events within 30 miles of Macon are included in standard service pricing. Events beyond that area may incur an additional travel fee based on distance, travel time, lodging requirements, and overall event logistics.

What does the venue need to provide? +

Clients are responsible for ensuring venue approval for beverage service and obtaining any permits, permissions, licenses, or other approvals required by the venue or local jurisdiction.

Reasonable access for setup, service, and breakdown must also be provided. Specific setup requirements will be confirmed during the planning process.

What happens if my event is outdoors and it rains? +

Outdoor events require adequate shelter and a safe service area in the event of inclement weather. A covered, level surface with access to electricity is required for outdoor bar setups.

We recommend having a weather contingency plan for severe weather conditions and will work with you on logistics during the planning process.

04
Responsible Hospitality

Safety & Service

How does The Traveling Tavern handle responsible alcohol service? +

Guest safety is always a priority. The Traveling Tavern reserves the right to refuse alcohol service to any guest who appears visibly intoxicated, underage, or otherwise unable to consume alcohol responsibly.

Are clients responsible for venue permits and alcohol requirements? +

Yes. Clients are responsible for ensuring that all venue requirements, permits, permissions, and approvals related to alcohol service have been obtained before the event.

This may include local or state licensing, venue-specific insurance requirements, and event permits depending on the location and circumstances of the event.

When should final guest counts and menu selections be confirmed? +

Final guest counts and menu selections should be confirmed before the event so we can accurately plan staffing, ingredients, bar supplies, and service requirements.

We'll work with you during the planning process to make sure these details are addressed well in advance of your event date.

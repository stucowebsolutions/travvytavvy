How booking works

The process is designed to keep planning simple while giving us the details needed to build a thoughtful, accurate quote.

01

Initial Consultation

We begin with a conversation about your event — the vision, guest count, and atmosphere you want to create. Whether you have every detail planned or just a feeling, we meet you where you are.

02

Custom Menu Design

Based on your preferences, season, and event style, we craft a custom beverage menu — signature cocktails, elevated mocktails, or both. Every recipe is designed specifically for your celebration.

03

Logistics & Planning

We coordinate setup details, timing, bar placement, and staffing. You receive a personalized alcohol purchasing guide tailored to your guest count and menu selections.

04

Event Day

We arrive early, set up a beautifully styled bar, and handle every detail — from garnish prep to glassware to guest service. You enjoy your event while we take care of the rest.

05

Breakdown & Departure

At the end of service, we break down the bar, pack out all equipment, and leave your venue clean. No mess, no stress — just memories.
